Is Hisingen affiliated with Polestar or Volvo?

No. Hisingen is independent open-source software, not affiliated with, endorsed by or maintained by Polestar or Volvo Car Corporation. Both names are trademarks of their respective owners, referenced only to describe compatibility.

What do I need to get started?

A Mac on macOS 14 or later and a vehicle account. Volvo works out of the box — sign in with your Volvo ID. Polestar uses your Polestar account directly. There is no Hisingen account.

Is the Polestar integration stable?

Polestar doesn't provide a supported public third-party API. Hisingen builds on the interfaces used by Homebrew and other solutions, which can change without notice — remote commands are experimental and marked as such. The Volvo integration uses official, documented APIs.

Where does my data go?

From your Mac to your car's provider — and nowhere else. Credentials live in the macOS Keychain, vehicle data is cached locally, and there is no Hisingen server or analytics. See the privacy policy.
