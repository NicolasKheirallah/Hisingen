Vehicle at a glance

Everything the car reports.
One click away.

The menu bar panel opens with the numbers you actually check. Behind them sit detail views for charging, health, history and everything else the car exposes — and nothing it doesn't.

Status at a glance.

Battery level, estimated range, lock state and charging progress — shown the moment the panel opens. Values the car hasn't reported recently are marked as such, never silently refreshed.

  • Battery, range and charging state
  • Lock, doors and windows
  • Vehicle health and service information
  • Last-updated freshness, always visible

Charging, with history.

Live charging detail — connection, power, current limit, target and estimated completion — plus a local record of past sessions with energy, duration and estimated cost. Figures are estimates from vehicle telemetry, and Hisingen labels them that way.

  • AC and DC charging state
  • Current limit and charge target
  • Session history with cost estimates
  • CSV export for your own analysis

Nothing invented, nothing hidden.

Doors, windows, hood, tailgate and charge flap are reported exactly as the car reports them. If a vehicle doesn't expose a value, Hisingen shows that — it never quietly assumes closed and locked.

  • Per-door and window state
  • Hood, tailgate and charge flap
  • Tyre pressure warnings
  • Alarm status where reported

Controls that know the car.

Where the vehicle and account allow it, Hisingen can start climate, lock or unlock, flash the lights and adjust charging. Controls a car can't perform don't appear — an unavailable command explains itself instead of failing silently.

  • Climate start and stop
  • Lock, unlock, horn and lights
  • Charging settings and schedules
  • Touch ID for sensitive commands

Remote functionality depends on vehicle, account and provider APIs. Polestar remote commands are experimental.

Supported cars

Made for Polestar and Volvo.

Two integrations, built on two very different APIs — and described honestly, including what each one can't do.

Native macOS

A real Mac app.
Not a web view.

Hisingen is built with AppKit and SwiftUI — small, fast, and at home on macOS. It uses the platform the way a Mac utility should.

System notifications
Charging, security and health events, with quick actions like Lock and Resume Schedule.
Apple Shortcuts
Battery, range and charging state — plus command handoffs — via App Intents.
Keychain
Credentials persist as separate, device-only Keychain items. Never in plain files.
Spotlight
Search your car's nickname for battery, range and charging — entirely local.
Login Items
Launch at startup through the standard macOS mechanism.
hisingen://
A local URL scheme for automation — lock, climate, charge target and more.

Is Hisingen affiliated with Polestar or Volvo?

No. Hisingen is independent open-source software, not affiliated with, endorsed by or maintained by Polestar or Volvo Car Corporation. Both names are trademarks of their respective owners, referenced only to describe compatibility.

What do I need to get started?

A Mac on macOS 14 or later and a vehicle account. Volvo works out of the box — sign in with your Volvo ID. Polestar uses your Polestar account directly. There is no Hisingen account.

Is the Polestar integration stable?

Polestar doesn't provide a supported public third-party API. Hisingen builds on the interfaces its own apps use, which can change without notice — remote commands are experimental and marked as such. The Volvo integration uses official, documented APIs.

Where does my data go?

From your Mac to your car's provider — and nowhere else. Credentials live in the macOS Keychain, vehicle data is cached locally, and there is no Hisingen server or analytics. See the privacy policy.
