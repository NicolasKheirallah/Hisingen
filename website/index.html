What the provider reports

Useful detail, not empty fields.

Hisingen adapts to battery-electric, plug-in hybrid and combustion vehicles. Irrelevant values are hidden, estimates are labelled, and unavailable data stays unavailable.

05 / ENERGY

Battery, range, fuel and charging.

Depending on the vehicle, see state of charge, fuel, charger connection, AC or DC type, power, current, voltage, target, limits and estimated completion. Charging history is a local estimate, not utility-grade metering.

06 / HEALTH

Health that explains a warning.

Service state, fluids, 12 V battery, lighting, tyres, odometer and trip information are grouped by the capabilities the provider exposes.

07 / CLIMATE

Climate is vehicle dependent.

Hisingen can show climate state, temperatures, timers and cabin information where available. A Polestar 2 should not be assumed to expose the same controls as a newer platform.

08 / CONTEXT

Location is optional.

Show the latest provider-reported position and open it in Apple Maps. Optional weather uses Open-Meteo and sends coordinates only when enabled.

Support is specific

Does Hisingen support your car?

Model recognition is only the beginning. Hisingen combines conservative model profiles with runtime capability observations and a six-hour freshness window.

Polestar

Polestar 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 are recognized. The integration follows current first-party client behavior because there is no supported public third-party vehicle-cloud API for this use case. It can change without notice.

Polestar integration notes ↗

Volvo

Volvo uses documented Connected Vehicle, Energy and Location APIs. Setup requires your own Volvo developer application and approved scopes where required.

Questions before download

The short version.

Is Hisingen official Polestar or Volvo software?+

No. Hisingen is independent open-source software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by Polestar Performance AB or Volvo Car Corporation.

Why are some features unavailable?+

Capabilities vary by model, model year, region, account role, vehicle state, API scopes and backend rollout. Hisingen reports those limits rather than guessing.

Are remote controls enabled by default?+

No. Remote controls are opt-in, capability-aware and subject to provider permissions. Sensitive operations can require Touch ID or the Mac password. A backend acknowledgement is not proof that the physical vehicle completed an action.

Does Hisingen track users?+

No. There is no analytics, advertising, developer-operated backend or usage telemetry. Optional location, weather and update-check requests are described in the privacy documentation.

Where are credentials stored?+

Polestar and Volvo sessions are stored locally in separate macOS Keychain entries using device-only accessibility. Hisingen does not claim ordinary credentials are Secure Enclave keys.

What does Volvo setup require?+

Volvo uses OAuth2 PKCE and documented developer APIs. You need your own Volvo developer application, a registered HTTPS callback and the required credentials or approved scopes.
