01 / At a glance

The useful parts of your vehicle, quietly close.

Hisingen lives in the macOS menu bar. It keeps a current, readable view of the vehicle you care about, then gets out of the way.

See what it shows ↓

Vehicle overview

One clear read on what matters now.

Battery, range, charging state, availability and vehicle identity are brought together in a native popover. Electric, hybrid and combustion vehicles get the fields that make sense for their powertrain.

  • 01Battery and range
  • 02Charging state and speed
  • 03Availability and freshness
  • 04Vehicle identity and build data

Charging intelligence

See the charge, not just the percentage.

Charging power, current, voltage, target, connection type and estimated completion time are surfaced when the vehicle reports them. Optional local session history can estimate energy and cost from your own tariff.

Cost and range-health figures are estimates, clearly labelled as such.

02 / Two providers

Polestar and Volvo.
Separate where it counts.

Both brands fit the same Hisingen experience, but their integrations remain independent under the hood. Your credentials and state never cross provider boundaries.

03 / Controls

Useful control.
Honest boundaries.

Climate, locks, windows, lighting and charging controls are shown only when the app has reason to believe the vehicle supports them. Standard distributed builds keep remote operations opt-in and protected.

A backend acknowledgement is not a guarantee that the vehicle executed a command.

Capability-aware by design

Hisingen adapts to the vehicle you actually have.

Capabilities are probed per vehicle and cached locally. Unsupported controls stay out of the way; backend-dependent features remain clearly best effort instead of becoming promises.

04 / Built for macOS

A proper Mac utility, not a web view in disguise.

Hisingen is one native AppKit and SwiftUI process. It runs in the menu bar, refreshes with an adaptive cadence and integrates with the parts of macOS that make a small utility feel at home.

01Menu bar and quick controls

Battery, range, charging-aware displays, context menus and global shortcuts.

02Siri, Shortcuts and App Intents

Ask for battery status or build simple workflows around supported actions.

03Deep links and notifications

hisingen:// links, native alerts, widgets and system light/dark appearance.

04Local, low-overhead operation

One process, no Hisingen server, no analytics bundle and no account system of its own.

05 / Privacy

Your credentials stay on your Mac.

Hisingen talks directly to Polestar and Volvo services using accounts you provide. It has no developer-operated backend, advertising, analytics or usage telemetry.

07 / Questions

The short version.

Which vehicles are supported?+

Polestar 1 through 6 are represented with conservative, model-aware profiles. Volvo support covers the models returned by Volvo's APIs, including XC40, XC60, XC90, S60, S90, V60, V90, C40, EX30, EX40, EX90 and ES90. Actual support is confirmed per vehicle where possible.

Is Hisingen an official Polestar or Volvo app?+

No. Hisingen is independent open-source software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by Polestar Performance AB or Volvo Car Corporation.

Why are some controls unavailable?+

Capabilities vary by model, model year, region, account role, vehicle state and backend rollout. Hisingen keeps unsupported or unverified controls hidden or disabled rather than implying they work.

Where are credentials stored?+

Polestar and Volvo credentials and session tokens are stored locally in separate macOS Keychain entries. They are protected with device-only accessibility and do not sync to iCloud Keychain.

Does Hisingen track users?+

No. Hisingen has no analytics, advertising, developer-operated backend or usage telemetry. Optional location, weather and update checks contact their respective services only when enabled.

What macOS version is required?+

The current package targets macOS 14 Sonoma or later and ships as a universal Apple Silicon and Intel app.

Is Hisingen free and open source?+

Yes. Hisingen is released under the MIT License. You can inspect the source, report issues and contribute through GitHub.
